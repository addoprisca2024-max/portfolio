About me

Marketing is about people first — platforms come second.

Hello! I'm Prisca Addo, an aspiring digital marketer training with Generation Ghana and dedicated to helping businesses grow through creative marketing strategies and meaningful online engagement.

I enjoy transforming ideas into impactful digital experiences by combining creativity with data-driven decision making — building a brand's online presence, creating engaging content, improving search visibility and developing campaigns that deliver measurable results.

Alongside marketing, I founded and run Prissy Importation, sourcing quality products for customers in Ghana. Running my own business taught me pricing, logistics, customer care and the value of trust — lessons I bring to every client project.

I believe successful marketing is about understanding people, solving problems and building lasting relationships between brands and their customers.
